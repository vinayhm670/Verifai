Scam Awareness

Fraud Prevention & Scam Awareness Center

Protect yourself from forged documents, payment scams, and corporate impersonation.

Unofficial & Lookalike Email Domains

Domain Spoofing

Scammers often use free webmail accounts (@gmail.com, @outlook.com) or deceptive domains like company-careers-hr.com to impersonate authentic recruiters.

VERIFAI Rule: Always check the domain header, verify independently on the official careers page, and never accept hiring from personal email addresses.

Recruitment Payment & Deposit Demands

Financial Extortion

Any request for 'laptop security deposits', 'interview processing charges', 'mandatory training fees', or 'stipend release bonds' is a classic hallmark of recruitment fraud.

VERIFAI Rule: Legitimate organizations never ask job seekers or interns to pay money upfront to secure an offer or receive their stipend.

Forged QR Codes & Fake Verifications

Static Link Manipulation

Scammers attach generic QR codes that link to fake Google Forms, unofficial websites, or static images mimicking corporate approval seals.

VERIFAI Rule: Always decode the QR code and ensure the destination is an authentic verification portal matching canonical registry records.

Phantom Hiring & Unrealistic Packages

High Urgency Trap

Offers extended without credible interviews, offering absurdly high compensation packages for freshers with urgent 24-hour acceptance ultimatums.

VERIFAI Rule: Reputable companies follow structured multi-stage assessments. Never rush into signing or paying due to artificial urgency.
